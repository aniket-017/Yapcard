APP NAME
Yapcard

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Turn your group chat into a recap. Everyone gets a title, a roast and a card.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Who talks the most in your group chat? Who disappears for weeks? Who texts at 2 a.m.? Yapcard reads your chat export and turns it into a recap your friends will actually want to share.

How it works
1. Export a chat from WhatsApp (Without media), or a Telegram chat as JSON.
2. Open it in Yapcard.
3. Tap through your recap, then reveal everyone's card.

What you get
• A story-style recap: total messages, your busiest hour, your loudest day, longest streak, top emojis and the words you can't stop saying.
• Guess-who questions before each reveal, so the whole group can play along.
• A title card for every member, like The Chief Yapper, The Ghost, The 2 a.m. Menace, The Essay Writer or The Instant Replier, each with a stat and a roast line.
• The power duo: the two people who reply to each other the fastest.
• Your group's personality, in one line.
• A group poster with everyone's title on one image.

Made to share
Every slide and card saves as a 9:16 image, sized for Instagram Stories and WhatsApp Status. Share yours, send the poster back to the group, and see who complains first.

Pick the roast level
Choose Friendly, Spicy or Savage, and switch any time. Roasts are about texting habits only: how much, how fast and how late you text.

Private by design
Your chat is read on your phone. The chat file is never uploaded or stored. If you turn on AI-written roasts, the app sends only the stats and a short sample of messages to write them, with names, phone numbers, emails and links removed first. Turn it off and nothing leaves your phone.

Works with group chats and one-to-one chats in any language.

Yapcard is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to WhatsApp, Meta or Telegram.
